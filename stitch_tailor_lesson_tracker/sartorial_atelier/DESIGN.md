## Brand & Style

This design system channels the quiet luxury, tactile precision, and heritage craftsmanship of a high-end bespoke tailoring atelier. It reconciles old-world Savile Row discipline with modern, minimalist editorial design. The target audience values provenance, bespoke fabrication, intentionality, and obsessive attention to detail.

The aesthetic philosophy balances tactile warmth with rigorous geometric order:
- **Artisanal Tactility:** Mimics raw woven textiles, hand-drawn tailor's chalk marks, pressed paper patterns, and basting stitches.
- **Editorial Balance:** Generous, unhurried negative space that frames content like a curated lookbook or bespoke cut sheet.
- **Crafted Restraint:** Avoids synthetic digital sheen, vibrant neon accents, or aggressive gradients. Depth is tactile, physical, and composed of organic textile layers.

## Colors

The color palette is grounded in the material workspace of the master clothier:
- **Primary (`#1B2432` - Midnight Wool):** A deep, saturated navy reminiscent of heavy worsted suiting fabric. Used for primary typography, authoritative structural strokes, and high-emphasis controls.
- **Secondary (`#C26D48` - Terracotta Basting):** A warm, burnt earth tone evoking tracing wax, pressed clay, and contrast edge dye. Reserved for focal interaction points, focused indicators, and status highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D1B280` - Tailor’s Chalk & Camel):** A soft, muted camel hue reflecting raw parchment, measuring tapes, and thread spools. Used for sub-accents, badge backgrounds, and warm borders.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` - Pressed Ivory / Raw Linen):** A tactile, warm paper ground that prevents stark digital glare. Applied to main application canvases, page backdrops, and muted component interiors.

### Supporting Tone Guidelines
- **Chalk Wash (`#F1ECE1`):** Applied to card containers, interactive inputs, and secondary layers to provide subtle surface distinction without introducing cold greys.
- **Thread Line (`#DDD5C7`):** The structural line color used for bespoke dividing rules, basting guides, and framing boundaries.
- **Bespoke Gold / Brass (`#8A6B3A`):** Low-frequency accent used strictly for insignia, master seals, and verified craft attributes.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary elegance of **Newsreader** with the clean, measured clarity of **Manrope**.

- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts:** Use Newsreader to anchor sections with grace, human craftsmanship, and historical authority. Utilize italic variations of Newsreader sparingly to denote fabric specifications, artisanal notes, or bespoke provenance.
- **Interface & Operational Data:** Use Manrope for high scannability across measurement forms, garment inventories, and functional navigation.
- **Micro-Labels & Technical Annotations:** Render small labels in uppercase Manrope with generous letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`), evoking tailor garment tags and technical swatch labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is informed by bespoke pattern-cutting papers and editorial broadsheets:
- **Grid Architecture:** Built on an asymmetric 12-column grid on desktop (max content container: `1320px`), reflowing to a 6-column system on tablet (`768px - 1024px`) and a 4-column system on mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Pacing & Breathing Room:** Generous page-level margins (`margin: 3rem`) mimic the wide margins of a pattern maker’s workbench. Dense clusters are reserved solely for spec lists and technical measurement sheets.
- **Rhythmic Stacking:** Component internal spacing relies consistently on modular increments of `0.25rem` (4px basis), using `space-md` for standard card interiors and `space-lg` for card layouts with editorial framing.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids digital elevation tiers in favor of physical textile and paper layering:

- **Surface Layering:** Stacking occurs via chromatic contrast rather than high z-index shadows. Surfaces step from the raw canvas (`#F9F6F0`) to warm ivory card sheets (`#FFFFFF` or `#FBF9F4`).
- **Tactile Shadows:** Shadows are warm-tinted, widely dispersed, and ultra-soft, reminiscent of heavy cardstock sitting beneath atelier morning light:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(27, 36, 50, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 36, 50, 0.03)`
  - *Floating Layer / Popover:* `0 4px 16px -4px rgba(27, 36, 50, 0.06), 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(27, 36, 50, 0.08)`
- **The Basting Stroke (Tailor Stitched Borders):** Primary containers and structural panels feature a delicate 1px border (`#DDD5C7`). Selected feature cards or drawer dividers incorporate an artisanal basting dash: a 1px dotted or 3px/3px dashed stroke that echoes guide threads.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses soft, pressed craftsmanship. It avoids purely brutalist sharp rectangles as well as overly playful pill geometry:
- **Standard Corners (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to form fields, small tags, tooltips, and action buttons. Reflects precision-cut cloth edges.
- **Container Corners (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to fabric swatches, measurement tiles, and dialog modals.
- **Hero Containers (`rounded-xl: 0.75rem` / 12px):** Applied to primary editorial panels and visual showcase modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep Midnight Wool (`#1B2432`) background, pressed ivory text (`#F9F6F0`), Manrope semi-bold typography. 1px tonal border in `#1B2432`. Hover shifts smoothly to an intentional deep navy tint (`#2A374A`).
- **Secondary Button (Linen & Basting):** Canvas ivory background, framed by a delicate 1px border (`#DDD5C7`). On hover, the border darkens to `#1B2432` with subtle camel wash background (`#F5EFE6`).
- **Accent Button:** Terracotta Basting (`#C26D48`) fill with ivory text, reserved exclusively for irreversible booking steps or high-priority calls to action.

### Chips & Tags
- Pill or slightly softened rectangular badges with a subtle 1px border.
- Textile classification tags use `#F1ECE1` background with uppercase `label-sm` text in `#1B2432`.
- Status indicators (e.g., "In Cutting", "Basted Fitting", "Finished") utilize small terracotta dot badges (`#C26D48`) with muted camel surrounds.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Background of `#FDFCF9` enclosed by a 1px perimeter in `#DDD5C7`.
- Focus state eliminates glowing outer halos; instead, it reinforces the border to 1.5px solid `#1B2432` or `#C26D48`, accompanied by an immediate, sharp label transition.
- Measurement inputs feature dedicated suffix labels (e.g., `cm`, `in`, `drop`) styled in low-contrast Newsreader italic.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkboxes: Clean 16px squares with soft corners (`0.125rem`). Unchecked uses a 1.5px border in `#DDD5C7`; checked fills with `#1B2432` revealing a sharp ivory thread-style checkmark.
- Radio buttons: Classic circular target with an inner terracotta core (`#C26D48`) upon selection.

### Cards & Swatch Panels
- **Editorial Garment Card:** White-to-linen surface (`#FFFFFF`), faint perimeter stitch border (`#E6E0D4`), soft ambient shadow. Typography features Newsreader headlines paired with Manrope spec lines.
- **Fabric Swatch Card:** Integrates a physical aspect-ratio frame, displaying fiber composition, mill origin, and weight tags directly beneath an edge-to-edge fabric image.

### Tailoring-Specific Components
- **The Basting Divider:** A specialized rule element that renders a faint, dashed horizontal line with a centered icon (scissors, needle eye, or monogram) breaking the stroke.
- **Measurement Matrix Sheet:** A high-density, clean-lined data grid styled like a bespoke ledger book, featuring subtle alternating row fills in `#F9F6F0` and `#F5EFE6`.
